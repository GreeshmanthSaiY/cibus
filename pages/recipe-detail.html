Andhra Pradesh

Pulihora (Andhra Tamarind Rice)

⏱️ Prep: 15 mins 🔥 Cook: 25 mins 🍽️ 4 Servings ⚡ Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients Checklist

  • 2 cups Cooked Sona Masuri Rice (cooled)
  • 1 large lemon-sized ball of Tamarind (soaked in warm water)
  • 1/4 cup Raw Peanuts
  • 1 tbsp Chana Dal (Bengal gram)
  • 1 tbsp Urad Dal (Split black gram)
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 2 sprigs Fresh Curry Leaves
  • 3-4 Slit Green Chilies
  • 3 Dried Red Chilies
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetida (Hing)
  • 3 tbsp Sesame or Peanut Oil
  • Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Cook rice until grains are fluffy and distinct; spread onto a wide tray with 1 tbsp sesame oil and turmeric powder to cool.

2

Extract thick tamarind pulp from soaked tamarind with 1 cup of warm water.

3

In a heavy pan, simmer the tamarind pulp with slit green chilies, salt, and turmeric until it thickens into a glossy paste.

4

In a separate pan, heat oil and roast peanuts until crunchy. Add mustard seeds, chana dal, urad dal, dried red chilies, hing, and fresh curry leaves.

5

Combine the thickened tamarind paste with the cooled rice gently, then pour the hot tempering over it and mix thoroughly.

6

Let the pulihora rest for at least 1 hour so the aromatic flavors infuse deeply before serving.

Bamboo Shoot Stir Fry

⏱️ Prep: 20 mins 🔥 Cook: 20 mins 🍽️ 3 Servings ⚡ Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients Checklist

  • 250g Fresh or Fermented Tender Bamboo Shoots (thinly sliced)
  • 2 tbsp Cold-pressed Mustard Oil
  • 4 Garlic cloves (crushed)
  • 3 Fresh Green Chilies (slit lengthwise)
  • 1/2 tsp Fenugreek Seeds (Methi)
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 medium Onion (thinly sliced)
  • Fresh Coriander or indigenous herbs for garnish
  • Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Boil sliced bamboo shoots in salted water for 10-15 minutes to eliminate natural bitterness, then drain thoroughly.

2

Heat mustard oil in a cast-iron wok until it begins to smoke gently, then lower heat.

3

Add fenugreek seeds and crushed garlic; sautÃ© until aromatic and golden.

4

Add sliced onions and green chilies, cooking until onions turn translucent.

5

Stir in the drained bamboo shoots, turmeric powder, and salt. SautÃ© on medium-high heat for 8-10 minutes.

6

Garnish with fresh herbs and serve hot alongside steamed sticky rice.

Assamese Duck Meat Curry (Kumura Dha)

⏱️ Prep: 25 mins 🔥 Cook: 50 mins 🍽️ 4 Servings ⚡ Difficulty: Hard

Ingredients Checklist

  • 800g Fresh Duck Meat (cut into bone-in curry pieces)
  • 300g White Gourd / Ash Gourd (Kumura), peeled and cubed
  • 3 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 2 large Onions (finely sliced)
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • 1 tsp Crushed Black Peppercorns
  • 1 tsp Cumin Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 2 Bay Leaves (Tejpatta)
  • 3 Green Cardamom pods and 1 inch Cinnamon
  • Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Heat mustard oil in a heavy-bottomed kadhai until smoking point, then add bay leaves, cardamom, and cinnamon.

2

Add sliced onions and sautÃ© until golden brown. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook out the raw aroma.

3

Add the duck meat, turmeric, salt, and cumin powder. Cook on high heat for 10 minutes until meat browns.

4

Lower heat, cover tightly, and allow duck to cook in its own juices for 20 minutes.

5

Add cubed ash gourd and freshly crushed black pepper; toss well to coat.

6

Pour in 2 cups of warm water, cover, and slow-cook until duck meat is fork-tender and curry is thick.

7

Serve piping hot with fragrant Joha rice.

Bihari Litti Chokha

⏱️ Prep: 30 mins 🔥 Cook: 35 mins 🍽️ 4 Servings ⚡ Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients Checklist

  • 2 cups Whole Wheat Flour (Atta)
  • 1.5 cups Roasted Gram Flour (Sattu)
  • 2 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 1 tsp Ajwain (Carom seeds) & Kalonji (Nigella seeds)
  • 4 Green Chilies and 1 inch Ginger (finely chopped)
  • 1/2 cup Fresh Coriander (chopped)
  • 1 large Round Eggplant (Brinjal) for Chokha
  • 3 ripe Tomatoes for Chokha
  • 2 Boiled Potatoes for Chokha
  • Desi Ghee for dipping
  • Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Knead a firm, pliable dough using wheat flour, a pinch of salt, 1 tsp oil, and water. Rest for 20 minutes.

2

For filling: Mix sattu with chopped green chilies, ginger, coriander, ajwain, kalonji, lemon juice, mustard oil, and salt with a splash of water until moist.

3

Form dough into smooth lemon-sized balls, cup them into hollow pockets, fill generously with sattu mixture, and seal tight.

4

Bake littis on a charcoal grill, tandoor, or preheated oven at 200Â°C for 25-30 minutes, turning periodically until outer crust is charred and crisp.

5

For chokha: Roast brinjal and tomatoes on open flame until skins are charred. Peel and mash together with boiled potatoes, raw mustard oil, chopped onions, green chilies, and salt.

6

Dip hot littis generously in melted desi ghee, crack them open slightly, and serve alongside smoky chokha.

  • 1/2 cup Cooked Leftover Rice (mashed)
  • 1/2 cup Chopped Fresh Fenugreek (Methi) or Spinach
  • 2 tbsp Oil
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1 tsp White Sesame Seeds (Til)
  • 2 Dried Red Chilies
  • 1 sprig Curry Leaves
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric & Hing
  • Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Combine rice flour, mashed rice, chopped greens, salt, turmeric, and warm water to form a soft dough.

2

Pinch small portions of dough and shape into cylindrical muthia rolls using your fingers.

3

Grease a steamer plate and steam muthias for 12-15 minutes until firm to the touch.

4

Heat oil in a wide pan; crackle mustard seeds, sesame seeds, dried chilies, and curry leaves.

5

Add steamed muthias to the tempering and pan-fry on medium heat until surfaces turn lightly golden and crisp.

6

Serve warm with spicy green coriander chutney.

Ingredients Checklist

  • 1 cup Fresh Grated Coconut
  • 6-8 Dried Kashmiri Red Chilies
  • 1 tbsp Coriander Seeds & 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 4 Garlic cloves & 1 inch Ginger
  • 4-5 Dried Kokum petals (soaked in warm water)
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 2 Green Chilies (slit)
  • 1 cup Thick Coconut Milk
  • Coconut Oil & Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Grind grated coconut, Kashmiri chilies, coriander seeds, cumin, garlic, ginger, and turmeric into a silky smooth paste with a little water.

2

Heat coconut oil in an earthenware pot (matka); add slit green chilies and the ground spice paste. SautÃ© gently until oil separates.

3

Add 1.5 cups of water and soaked kokum with its water; bring the curry to a gentle simmer.

4

Gently slide in fish fillets and coconut milk; simmer on low heat for 6-8 minutes without stirring vigorously to avoid breaking the fish.

5

Season with salt and let rest for 15 minutes before serving with hot steamed Goan red rice.

Gujarat

Khaman Dhokla

⏱️ Prep: 15 mins 🔥 Cook: 20 mins 🍽️ 4 Servings ⚡ Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients Checklist

  • 1.5 cups Besan (Gram flour)
  • 1 tbsp Semolina (Sooji)
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 1 tsp Ginger-Green Chili Paste
  • 1 tsp Eno Fruit Salt or Baking Soda
  • 1/4 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tbsp Sugar
  • For tempering: 1 tbsp Oil, 1 tsp Mustard Seeds, 1 tbsp Sesame Seeds, 4 Slit Green Chilies, 1 sprig Curry Leaves, 1/2 cup Water with 1 tsp Sugar
  • Garnish: Fresh grated coconut and coriander

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Whisk besan, sooji, turmeric, ginger-chili paste, lemon juice, sugar, salt, and water into a smooth, lump-free batter.

2

Grease a steaming tin and boil water in the steamer.

3

Right before pouring, stir in Eno fruit salt and 1 tsp water; whisk in one direction until batter doubles in volume and becomes airy.

4

Pour immediately into the tin and steam on high heat for 18-20 minutes until a toothpick inserted comes out clean.

5

In a small pan, prepare tempering by crackling mustard seeds, sesame seeds, curry leaves, and green chilies in oil, then pour in sugar water.

6

Cut steamed dhokla into neat squares, pour the warm tempering water evenly to keep it moist, and garnish with coconut and coriander.

Haryanvi Bajra Khichdi

⏱️ Prep: 20 mins 🔥 Cook: 45 mins 🍽️ 4 Servings ⚡ Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients Checklist

  • 1 cup Pearl Millet (Bajra), crushed or coarsely ground
  • 1/2 cup Yellow Moong Dal (split)
  • 5 cups Water
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetida (Hing)
  • 3 tbsp Desi Ghee
  • Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Pound bajra lightly in a mortar or pulse coarsely in a blender, then husk to remove chaff.

2

Wash bajra and moong dal together thoroughly.

3

In a heavy pot or pressure cooker, bring water to a boil, then add the bajra and dal mixture with salt and turmeric.

4

Cook on low heat, stirring frequently to prevent sticking, until grains are thoroughly soft and porridge-like (about 4 whistles in pressure cooker).

5

Heat ghee, temper with cumin seeds and hing, and stir into the hot khichdi.

6

Serve piping hot with fresh curd, jaggery, and extra spoons of golden ghee.

Jharkhandi Dhooska

⏱️ Prep: 4 hrs soaking 🔥 Cook: 30 mins 🍽️ 4 Servings ⚡ Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients Checklist

  • 1 cup Raw Rice
  • 1/2 cup Chana Dal (Bengal gram)
  • 1/4 cup Urad Dal (Split black gram)
  • 2 Green Chilies & 1 inch Ginger
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric & Hing
  • Oil for deep frying
  • Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Wash and soak rice, chana dal, and urad dal together in water for 4-5 hours.

2

Drain and grind into a thick, smooth pouring batter with green chilies, ginger, and minimal water.

3

Stir in cumin seeds, turmeric, hing, and salt. Whisk vigorously for 3 minutes to aerate.

4

Heat oil in a deep kadhai on medium heat.

5

Pour a small ladle of batter directly into the center without stirring; it will puff up like a poori.

6

Flip and fry until both sides are crisp and golden brown.

7

Drain on kitchen paper and serve hot with spicy aloo-chana sabzi.

Karnataka Bisi Bele Bath

⏱️ Prep: 20 mins 🔥 Cook: 35 mins 🍽️ 4 Servings ⚡ Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients Checklist

  • 1 cup Sona Masuri Rice
  • 1/2 cup Toor Dal (Pigeon pea)
  • 1.5 cups Diced Mixed Vegetables (Carrot, Beans, Peas, Shallots)
  • 3 tbsp Bisi Bele Bath Masala Powder
  • 1 small lemon-sized Tamarind (extracted)
  • 3 tbsp Desi Ghee
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds & 2 dried red chilies
  • 1 sprig Curry Leaves & 12 Cashews
  • Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Pressure cook rice and toor dal together with 4 cups water and turmeric until very soft.

2

In a pot, boil mixed vegetables in tamarind extract and salt until tender.

3

Dissolve Bisi Bele Bath powder in 1/2 cup water and stir into the simmering vegetables.

4

Add cooked rice and dal mixture; stir well, adding warm water to achieve a rich, semi-thick consistency.

5

Heat ghee in a pan, fry cashews, mustard seeds, red chilies, and curry leaves until fragrant.

6

Pour hot tempering over the bath, stir well, and serve hot with potato chips or boondi raita.

Kerala Appam with Stew

⏱️ Prep: 8 hrs fermenting 🔥 Cook: 25 mins 🍽️ 4 Servings ⚡ Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients Checklist

  • 2 cups Raw Rice (soaked 4 hours)
  • 1 cup Fresh Grated Coconut
  • 1/2 cup Cooked Rice
  • 1/2 tsp Active Dry Yeast (or toddy)
  • 1 tbsp Sugar
  • For Stew: 2 cups Mixed Vegetables, 1.5 cups Thin Coconut Milk, 1/2 cup Thick Coconut Milk, Whole Spices, Green Chilies, Coconut Oil, Curry Leaves
  • Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Grind soaked rice, grated coconut, and cooked rice with water into a smooth batter.

2

Dissolve yeast and sugar in 2 tbsp warm water, mix into batter, and ferment for 8 hours until frothy.

3

Add salt and a pinch of sugar; batter should be of pouring consistency.

4

Heat an appachatti (curved appam pan). Pour a ladle of batter into the center, swirl the pan to coat sides, cover, and steam on medium heat for 2-3 minutes.

5

The edges will turn golden and crisp while center remains fluffy.

6

For stew: Simmer vegetables with whole spices, green chilies, and thin coconut milk until soft, then finish with thick coconut milk and curry leaves tempered in coconut oil.

7

Serve warm appams with aromatic stew.

  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds & 1 sprig Curry Leaves
  • For assembly: 1 cup Spicy Farsan / Chiwda, Chopped Raw Onions, Lemon wedges, Coriander, 8 Buttered Pav buns
  • Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Boil sprouted matki with a pinch of turmeric and salt until just cooked but holding shape; drain and reserve liquid.

2

Heat oil generously in a kadhai; add mustard seeds and curry leaves.

3

Add onions and sautÃ© until deep golden brown, then add ginger-garlic paste and tomatoes until mushy.

4

Add misal masala, red chili powder, and salt. Cook until bright red oil ('tarri') floats on top.

5

Add boiled sprouts and 3 cups of water; simmer vigorously for 15 minutes to form rich spicy gravy.

6

Assembly: Ladle sprouts into bowls, top generously with spicy farsan and fresh onions, pour thin fiery tarri over, and serve with warm buttered pav and lemon.

For roti: Knead makki atta with warm water into soft dough. Pat gently between damp palms or parchment paper into round flatbreads.

6

Cook on a medium hot tawa with generous ghee until golden speckled on both sides.

7

Serve piping hot with a dollop of white butter (makhan) and jaggery.

  • 1 cup Fried Onions (Birista)
  • 1/4 cup Saffron Milk
  • 1/4 cup Melted Desi Ghee
  • Whole Spices (Shahi jeera, star anise, cardamom, mace, cinnamon, cloves)
  • Whole Wheat Dough to seal the pot
  • Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Marinate meat for at least 2 hours with yogurt, biryani spices, ginger-garlic paste, fresh herbs, and salt.

2

Boil water with whole spices and salt; cook soaked basmati rice until 70% done, then drain immediately.

3

In a thick-bottomed handi, layer marinated meat at the base.

4

Spread the 70% cooked rice evenly over the meat.

5

Top with fried onions, saffron milk, chopped mint, coriander, and generous spoons of ghee.

6

Seal the rim with wheat dough and cover tightly with a heavy lid.

7

Cook on high heat for 10 minutes, then place on a hot iron tawa on low heat for 35 minutes (dum).

8

Rest for 15 minutes before unsealing. Gently fluff layers to reveal aromatic long rice and tender meat, served with mirchi ka salan and dahi raita.

Bengali Kosha Mangsho

⏱️ Prep: 30 mins 🔥 Cook: 60 mins 🍽️ 4 Servings ⚡ Difficulty: Hard

Ingredients Checklist

  • 800g Tender Goat Mutton (curry cuts)
  • 1/2 cup Mustard Oil
  • 3 large Onions (finely sliced)
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • 1/2 cup Whisked Yogurt
  • 1 tbsp Kashmiri Chili Powder & 1 tsp Turmeric
  • Whole Spices: 2 Bay leaves, 4 Cardamom, 1 inch Cinnamon, 4 Cloves
  • 1 tsp Sugar (caramelized for rich dark color)
  • 4 Halved Fried Potatoes
  • Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Marinate mutton with yogurt, 1 tbsp mustard oil, turmeric, and red chili powder for 2 hours.

2

Heat remaining mustard oil in a heavy kadhai until smoking; add 1 tsp sugar and allow to caramelize into deep golden brown.

3

Add whole spices and sliced onions; sautÃ© patiently on medium heat until dark mahogany brown.

4

Add ginger-garlic paste and cook out raw aroma. Add marinated mutton and fry on medium-high heat for 15 minutes.

5

Cover and 'koshano' (slow roast-fry) on low flame, stirring every 5 minutes until meat turns deep dark brown and oil releases cleanly.

6

Add fried potato halves and 1.5 cups warm water; simmer on low heat until mutton is melt-in-mouth tender and gravy is thick and clinging to meat.

7

Serve hot with fluffy luchi, Bengali sweet pulao, or steamed rice.
